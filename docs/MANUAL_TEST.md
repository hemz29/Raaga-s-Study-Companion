# Manual test script: the full daily loop

Run on the latest Chrome, Edge and Safari on a laptop. Use a fresh profile or **Parent → Erase everything**.

## Day 1
1. **First run.** Greeting says "Hello, Raaga". Pick each world and check that the look changes. Receive the egg.
2. **Plan.** Open Plan and mark Maths and History "In portion", one Physics chapter "Probably", then press *Make my draft plan*. Check: ≤ 3 blocks a day, Maths every weekday, a Sunday boss fight, History revision touches. Drag a block to another day, press *Re-spread*, and check that the moved block stays (📌). Add 4 blocks to one day and check that the creature warns gently. Open *Want Mum to take a look?* and print.
3. **Launch.** Study shows today's suggestions. Swap one subject, then start.
4. **Detective briefing.** Shown before the Maths block. *Skip* needs a second tap. Ticking all items and picking an answer length enables the start button.
5. **Timer.** Pause and resume work. *Finished, log it* is disabled until 80% of the block. *Stop block* needs a confirm and the block doesn't count.
6. **Gate.** Paste is blocked. A 4-word point → "stretch it". "the king was very kind to all the people" → coaching question. The same point twice → duplicate. Valid points turn green. Save needs at least one exercise type.
7. **Break.** 5-minute countdown, skippable.
8. **Hatch.** After the 2nd gated block the egg wobbles, cracks and flashes, the creature is revealed, and Raaga names it.
9. **Mistake.** *I caught a mistake* → category + note under 5 words is refused; a valid note → sparkle and monocle cosmetic.
10. **Wrap-up.** Shows play time parts (15 base, +5 mistake), capped at 40. *Tell Mum* shows the exact text, lets her add a note and sends (on Vercel). Check that the email arrives.
11. **Rooms.** Art studio opens. Draw, undo/redo, save, pin to home, set a drawing as the Hatchling art. The minutes counter goes down. At 0, "that's enough adventure for today".

## Day 2 (change the system date or wait)
12. The recall quiz appears first with 3–5 questions from yesterday: answer, reveal, rate. A score ≥ 80% adds +10 min.
13. The home dots show 1 active day. If yesterday's report wasn't sent, the creature is sleepy and offers to send it.

## Other checks
- Light-day token: single block, 3 takeaways, token shows as used this week.
- Parent PIN: set, lock, unlock. Pause → creature peacefully asleep, Study disabled.
- Exam week (test date within 7 days): light-day setting half/off is applied.
- Backup download → Erase everything → Restore → everything is back, including drawings.
- CSV exports open in a spreadsheet.
- Offline: after first load, turn the network off and reload. The app still works.
- Mute toggle silences all sounds.
